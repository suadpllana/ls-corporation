Frequently Asked Questions

Here you find the most common answers about our web design and website rental service. Have another question? Contact us.

Do I own the domain?

The domain is registered in your name and remains yours forever. If you decide to leave LS Corporation, the domain will be transferred to you completely free.

Can I cancel the subscription whenever I want?

Yes, absolutely. There are no long-term contracts or penalties. You can cancel every month with 30 days advance notice. We will help you export your data.

Can I change the plan at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade every month. The change takes effect in the next billing cycle at no extra cost.

What happens if our website has technical issues?

Our team monitors websites 24/7. For Business and Enterprise plans, response time is within 2 hours.

Can I make changes after launch?

Yes. Each plan includes monthly changes at no extra cost (Basic: 3, Business: 8, Enterprise: unlimited). Urgent changes are handled within 24 hours.
